About Our Initiative

The Rohingya AI Language Initiative is a community-focused project connected with Rohingya Education School. Our goal is to build reliable digital resources that help preserve the Rohingya language and support future language technology research.

We welcome carefully reviewed vocabulary, example sentences, translations, stories, and educational materials contributed by the community.

Our Mission: Preserve the Rohingya language, support education, and make quality language resources available for researchers and technology developers.
